This is a simple game made with Python, based off of Joseph Distler's Roll.

The goal is simple: You get a deck of 52 cards, and draw each card 1 at a time. Cards give points based off of their ranks,
as well as combo for things like drawing consecutive pairs, drawing sequential ranks, etc.

The point of the game is to draw and try to get the highest score. As of now, everything is determined by chance.

At some point, there will be a shop with items and upgrades, not dissimilar from 'Roll'. I would also like to increase the
card amount by a ton (possibly 2600, as that is an even 50 decks and also closely matches Roll's 2500 rolls), but as of now,
it would not be fun at all, since the only thing you can do right now is type 'd' to draw a card. At the point of multiple 
decks worth of cards, there will be an autodraw feature.

I will also try my best to add graphics to the game using pygame. For now, it's a completely terminal based game, but there is
nice colored text when you get combos and whatnot.
